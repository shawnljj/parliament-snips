Committee of Supply debate on the Ministry of Social and Family Development budget

Committee of Supply5 March 2026 – 6 March 2026 budget-2930, budget-2934

A Committee of Supply debate on the Ministry of Social and Family Development budget, opened by Mr Xie Yao Quan moving a $100 reduction to Head I of the Estimates, with MPs raising policy asks and ministers setting out MSF plans.

The transcript sets out changes including a new $500 quarterly Partnership Payout for all ComLink+ families, a rise in the Student Care Fee Assistance income threshold from $4,500 to $6,500 benefiting around 13,000 students, and from January 2027 a higher gross monthly household income ceiling of $15,000 for childcare and Kindergarten Fee Assistance. It also states that from July 2026 subsidies for residential and community disability services will rise by up to 15 and 10 percentage points, and that filing Form 1 of an LPA will be free for citizens.
